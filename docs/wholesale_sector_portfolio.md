# Sector exposure timing and observed portfolio loss review

Run `examples/wholesale_sector_portfolio.py` or native `run_wholesale_sector_portfolio()`. Both accept `data` and `verify_reference`; R also accepts `engine_path`. The shared `specialized_portfolio_review` engine prepares the portfolio, computes concentration and performs LGD/EAD backtests. Seven additional exclusive event-loss calculations retain each claim's native currency.

The seven representative contracts explicitly schedule 20% principal repayment on 1 May 2024 after 61 actual/365 days at 5%. Default exposure before the payment is `P + 0.05*P*61/365`; after the scheduled payment it is `0.8*P + interest`. The observed ledger instead records only 10% principal repaid, so actual EAD is `0.9*P + interest`. Principal is chosen transparently to reconcile that bridge with each previously recorded sector EAD. Partial repayment and default ordering are stated; the forecast is not rewritten to equal the observed balance. This is a supplied contract scenario, not an assertion that all instruments in a sector share its amortization terms.

Conditional net recovery forecasts on 1 September are independent prospective assumptions, known at the March forecast date. Both these forecasts and actual recoveries use the same 5% annual discount convention over 123 days from default. Closed LGD therefore compares like with like. The zero-discount open-workout diagnostic is deliberately not used as an observed LGD or silently mixed into this comparison. Economic forecast loss is scheduled EAD minus discounted conditional net recovery. Two exclusive states, performing and default, integrate that loss against the actual frozen standalone PD from the sector model. Support and transfer event overlays are not added implicitly.

The sovereign claim remains USD in its contract schedule and event loss. For the EUR portfolio and monetary backtests only, both forecast and observed amounts are translated once at a declared fixed 0.9 EUR/USD contract assumption known in March. All other rates are one. This is a constant reference translation, not an FX stress or hedge claim. Dimensionless LGDs remain unchanged while their EAD weights use EUR consistently.

Portfolio positions use gross exposure, no fabricated collateral benefit, and explicit default-group policy. No graph edge means no contractual cross-default link; it does not establish statistical independence. Concentration HHI uses the seven EUR position weights. LGD bias is default-EAD weighted; EAD bias counts each defaulted facility once. Both have independent arithmetic oracles. Seven observations support these deterministic diagnostics, not an asymptotic uncertainty claim.

The shared engine takes graph information dates from the explicit portfolio contract, preserving the original specialized fixture's 2023 behavior and permitting these March2024 sector positions. Every source model hash, conditional cash forecast, FX assumption and observed EAD bridge is included in the resource.
