# Validation of frozen selection candidates

`selection_bias_sensitivity(candidates, evaluation_rows, selection_results, *, design, reference_model_id, uncertainty='none', cluster_resamples=None, confidence_level=.95, outcome_comparability=None, tolerance=1e-10, context=None)` compares already frozen predictions on the same through-the-door applications. It never fits an outcome or selection model and never chooses a lending policy.

The standard dated validation design and prediction-row contract apply. Each row additionally declares `application_id`, Boolean `funded`, `label_origin`, `outcome`, `known_at`, `target`, and `complete_through` for negative labels. A whole target horizon must mature before any label contributes, including a known early default. An incomplete negative or unavailable label remains excluded. Funding is distinct from an offer or acceptance. Every candidate has `model_id`, `model_hash`, `training_known_at`, explicit `training_entities`, and exactly one `{application_id, pd, known_at}` prediction per application. Training/evaluation entity or time contamination is excluded through `prepare_reject_evaluation`; frozen predictions must be available at the application index and after training.

Actual funded own-contract labels, authorized exploration, synthetic engineering truth, and explicitly transported external proxies have separate metric tables. Pseudo/hypothetical outcomes never enter these tables. Every table contains weighted Brier, log loss, calibration error (observed minus predicted), and tie-aware AUC/Gini. Single-class discrimination is not estimable. Paired contrasts are candidate minus reference on exactly the same scoped observations. Optional supplied whole-cluster draws give type-7 percentile intervals conditional on all frozen models. A contrast with an unidentifiable bootstrap replicate has no reported interval. These are descriptive comparisons, with no implicit multiple-testing acceptance rule.

Each optional selection result declares `result_id`, `kind` (`IPW`, `BOUNDS`, `MNAR_SHIFT`, `AIPW`), frozen `model_id`, exact `population_id`, target and ordered `application_ids`, the public arithmetic function's `inputs`, and its raw `result` value. `observed`, explicit `target_weights`, and `outcomes` must match mature eligible factual funded cases in the validation population. Synthetic/proxy labels cannot silently replace missing own-contract outcomes. MNAR baseline PDs must equal the candidate's frozen predictions. AIPW nuisance lineage binds the actual ordered entities and dates; its existing out-of-fold checks remain mandatory. The validator recomputes this arithmetic and compares the supplied result recursively with absolute/relative `tolerance`. A discrepancy blocks artifact reconciliation; it does not change or refit the artifact.

Positive target mass with zero observation propensity is `NOT_IDENTIFIED_ZERO_SUPPORT`, with a null arithmetic result. An observed case with zero propensity is contradictory input. IPW trimming can remove unsupported mass, but its output states the excluded mass and changed estimand. Effective sample size is the public IPW squared-sum/sum-of-squares diagnostic. IPW weighted factual metrics remain conditional on MAR and positivity; uncertainty in estimated propensities is not included. Bounds and MNAR surfaces retain their assumptions. AIPW preserves its clustered influence uncertainty and nuisance assumptions; this is separate from paired prediction bootstrap uncertainty. No scenario identifies new-policy performance merely because its arithmetic reconciles.

For four equally weighted applications, funded outcomes 0 and 1 and two unfunded cases give unrestricted population-risk bounds [0.25, 0.75]. PDs [0.2, 0.8] have funded Brier 0.04; [0.4, 0.6] have 0.16. Observation propensities 0.5 give two weights of 2 and ESS 2. Missing-case baseline risk 0.6 at zero log-odds shift gives population risk 0.55, an assumption-conditioned value rather than an observed rejection default rate.

The distinction between financing and acceptance, missing-data assumptions and invalid self-generated test labels follows [Ehrhardt et al., *Reject Inference Methods in Credit Scoring*, sections 1–2](https://adimajo.github.io/assets/publications/rejectInference.pdf). This implementation uses default=1; the paper's introductory good/bad convention differs. See [selection methods](selection_bias.md) for the underlying estimators and their sources.
