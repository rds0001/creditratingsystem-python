# Specialized asset-finance contracts

`examples/specialized_asset_finance.py` (`run(data=None, verify_reference=True)`) and the native R example (`run_specialized_asset_finance(data=NULL, verify_reference=TRUE)`) execute five product families and sixteen economically distinct variants. All parameters and training observations are synthetic, openly specified in `specialized_asset_finance.json`. Pass a resource-shaped object to replace them with your own explicit contracts. Reference assertions are valid only for the supplied world; set `verify_reference=False`/`FALSE` for different economic inputs. Neither function writes to installed resources.

| Product | Variants and distinct assumptions |
|---|---|
| Commercial real estate | Existing fixed rent; development with limited equity and delayed completion; two tenants with one lease expiring without automatic reletting |
| Project finance | Construction delay and capped support; operating availability/maintenance; 80% merchant pricing; fixed contracted offtake with buyer default |
| Aircraft finance | Single aircraft; two aircraft sharing one lessee; lease-backed asset with six-month remarketing delay and remaining return-condition repairs |
| Ship finance | Time charter expires into spot exposure; 90% spot earnings; newbuild with milestone spending, incomplete collateral and capped refund support |
| Equipment leasing | Single lease; two obsolete assets sharing one lessee; vendor guarantee expiring before the terminal default claim |

The actual VAR(1) fit uses 2016–2023 annual observations. The independently derived intercept is −1/7, slope −2/3 and innovation variance 0.31292517006802717. A genuine temporally split log-link satellite fit gives coefficients 0 and 0.2. Eighteen fixed-weight scenarios combine a three-point Gaussian innovation tensor with an explicitly declared common lessee event. They are quadrature/stress paths, not independent Monte Carlo trials or empirical validation observations. Asset factors and operating price factors are rounded to twelve decimals under the published scenario-precision contract before hashing. Inputs in currency units are not rounded.

A separate 27-node equilibrium tensor initializes the stable Gaussian VAR from its stationary distribution. Both future dates have mean −3/35 and variance 0.5632653061224489. This is the declared TTC reference law, with its stability and time-homogeneity assumptions preserved. Transaction risk remains PIT, conditional on the forecast state; its path average is never renamed TTC.

The shared transaction engine invokes debt schedules, operating cash flows, asset values, contract-counterparty simulation, liquidity waterfalls, first-default detection, recovery waterfalls and simulation risk. Public `content_fingerprint` seals each recipe, cube and parameter snapshot. Each obligor has a distinct legally pooled cash account. Actual interest is paid before principal, unpaid claims persist, reserves cannot be spent twice, and collateral is recovered only at first default. A maturity shortfall alone does not establish regulatory default: the declared joint liquidity/asset-insufficiency test or continuously material arrears over 90 days must hold. ACT/365F includes the 366-day second period.

Fixed receipts are already included once in operating cash flow; counterparty failure subtracts only those receipts. Merchant shares retain market repricing. Shared lessees create one event for several leases. Market depreciation and technical obsolescence affect asset value, not already-due receivables. Scheduled maintenance expenditure is distinct from unpaid return-condition work. Construction completion multipliers value incomplete assets explicitly, without assuming early delivery or unlimited equity.

Equipment lessee PD comes from an actual frequency-weighted scorecard and independent mature holdout calibration. The two training leverage groups have default rates 0.1 and 0.3. The application has leverage 2, giving 0.1 by independent binomial algebra. The fitted prediction is rounded to eight decimal places for the frozen counterparty contract; the unrounded estimate is checked within 1e-8. This one-year lessee event is distinct from the two-year borrowing cash-pool default.

Guarantees apply to each mutually exclusive first-default path, after collateral loss valuation, under explicit caps and due dates. A separate synthetic guarantor curve has cumulative PD 0.1 at year one and 0.2 at year two. The guarantor is independent of the macro/lessee path under this declared law. `allocate_guarantee_coverage`, `joint_default_probability`, `apply_guarantee_risk` and a complete exclusive-state loss partition reconcile the protection. Conditional loss after protection is `loss − (1−q) min(cap, max(loss,0))` only for eligible dates. Borrower PD never changes. The vendor guarantee has expired for every modeled default claim and therefore provides no loss reduction. These first-default-date payment conventions must be replaced when real legal contracts differ.

Independent scalar accounting recomputes all 288 scenario ledgers, principal and interest paid, arrears clocks, collateral ageing, first-default dates and discounted capped recovery. Weighted default and loss totals reconcile separately to the joint path-loss API. Every variant has its own successful route and a missing-verified-contract rejection. Default package tests execute five representative contracts with real fits; `CREDITRATINGSYSTEM_EXTENDED_TESTS=true` runs all sixteen. The full example always runs all variants unless the caller supplies a subset.

This example establishes product-component evidence. It does not by itself establish local review/publication history, monitoring, observed workout validation or every lifecycle phase for these products. Hidden reference algebra is segregated under `hidden_truth` and used only by assertions, never model fitting or scenario generation.
