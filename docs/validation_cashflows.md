# Dated collections validation

`collections_backtest` evaluates frozen repayment, recovery and collection-cost forecasts over declared fixed horizons. It consumes a sealed `build_collections_episodes` history at the evaluation cutoff, forecasts, dated cashflows, assignments/offers and a separate cash-capture coverage table. Forecast rows carry the common validation model, parameter, population, split, entity, cluster and horizon identities; add `account_id`, `episode_id`, `index_action_id` and `predicted_cashflows`.

Cashflow records have unique `cashflow_id`, explicit `kind` (`REPAYMENT`, `RECOVERY`, `COST`), nonnegative `amount`, matching `currency` and `occurred_at`; observed records also have `episode_id` and `known_at`. Categories must be mutually exclusive in the supplied ledger. Forecast cash dates lie in the inclusive forecast-as-of to horizon-end window. Capture records declare `episode_id`, `observed_from`, `observed_through`, `known_at`. A negative or zero cashflow outcome requires independently complete capture through the whole horizon; episode state-change coverage alone is insufficient. Unknown or late-known cash is excluded from historical replay. An open episode remains open even when a fixed-horizon observation is complete.

The selected index action must be a historically known `ASSIGNED` or `OFFERED` action matching the action embedded in its sealed episode. Eventual acceptance or performance is not substituted for pre-action assignment. Action-group comparisons describe factual outcomes; they do not identify treatment effects under selective assignment or uptake.

Declare a discount contract with `method='flat_effective_annual'`, `day_count='ACT_365F'`, `annual_rate > -1`, `currency`, `known_at` and `reference`. Its knowledge date precedes every forecast. A cashflow of amount A occurring d actual days after forecast is valued as `A * (1 + annual_rate)**(-d/365)`. Currency must agree with the validation design and any calculation context. No future fitted curve or implicit exchange rate is introduced.

Outputs contain weighted bias (prediction minus observed), MAE and RMSE for discounted repayments, recoveries, costs and net proceeds; net is repayment plus recovery minus cost. Recovery timing is the undiscounted recovery-amount-weighted mean number of days, scored only when both forecast and observed recovery mass are positive. Zero recovery does not create a zero-day timing observation. Coverage, excluded forecasts, cash identities and episode/action traces are retained.

Use `uncertainty='none'`, or `cluster_bootstrap` with caller-supplied shared cluster resamples. Percentile intervals condition on the frozen forecasts and use the same sampled clusters across metrics and action groups. If a resample has no eligible paired recovery timing, its interval is non-estimable. Repeated forecasts require a defensible independent cluster definition. These results are fixed-horizon factual assessments, not ultimate-recovery estimates, causal comparisons or release approval.

For two complete open episodes with predicted recoveries 50 and 40 and known recoveries 40 and 0, recovery bias is 25. If the first predicted recovery arrives on day 14 and its actual recovery on day 19, paired timing bias is -5 days; the second episode has no timing label. Two equally weighted bootstrap draws selecting only each episode give recovery biases 10 and 40 and a type-7 95% lower percentile of 10.75.
