# Contractual horizons and observed settlement

`examples/wholesale_commitment_observed.py:run(data=None, verify_reference=True, artifact_paths=None)` and native `run_wholesale_commitment_observed` (additional optional `engine_path`) evaluate two separately dated synthetic populations. Inputs can replace the packaged JSON; `artifact_paths` maps model resource names to frozen files. Both return `cohorts` with observation windows, mature/default counts, Brier score, recovery episodes and discounted closed-workout LGD.

The revolving cohort starts 1 January 2024, ends 31 January, and retains active zero-drawn commitments in the risk set. The defaulted working-capital line actually draws EUR60; this does not change the independently estimated conditional EAD50 in the component model. The leveraged cohort runs to the contractual balloon on 1 January 2026: two calendar years include leap day, hence 731 days. Its residual claim is principal120 + final interest12 − actual available payment18 = EUR114. Positive operating cash flow does not fund full maturity. Each cohort contains 20 mature accounts, one coverage-censored account and one competing early exit. Defaults are respectively 2 and 6. The two targets are never pooled as equivalent outcomes.

Separate synthetic servicing records supply defaults and actual recovery/cost payments. Revolver settlement is 31 May 2024: net42−3 discounted 121 days from default. Leveraged settlement is 1 July 2026: net95−8 discounted181 days. Both use a declared effective annual 5% default-date valuation. Final LGD is `1 - net_recovery / 1.05^(days/365) / EAD`. Legal closure is distinct from a cure; an OPEN episode cannot report finalized economic LGD. Observation coverage becomes known after the horizon and model/calibration inference uses only information available at origination.

The helper reloads and verifies model seals, recalculates PD with the correct hierarchical calibration group, and checks every stored prediction and exact observation horizon even when optional reference assertions are disabled. Tests include a tampered forecast and an incorrect 730-day leveraged horizon. Financial algebra, counts and censoring have independent hand oracles; complete Python/native output parity includes all seals.
