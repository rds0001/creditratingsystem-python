# Synthetic corporate lifecycle

Run `examples/wholesale_corporate_lifecycle.py`, or source the installed R example and call `run_wholesale_corporate_lifecycle()`. All financial inputs, historical cohorts and contracts are packaged in `wholesale_corporate_lifecycle.json`. The companion corporate component example supplies five distinct segment/instrument variants. This lifecycle follows one obligor, CO-001, through the eight product phases; it does not assert that every instrument has the same recovery process.

The origination model is fitted to observed frequency-weighted defaults, with a frozen 365-day PHYSICAL/PIT target. Dated statements select the profile and local registered model. The declared reviewed budget has cashflow 25 against service 10, with supplied legal eligibility and operational clearance. The assessment becomes stale after the financial deterioration event. A missed payment of 8 due March 15 is 31 days overdue on April 15. A proposed restructuring is an event, not an executed contract amendment. The default event is observed May 1. A settled claim supersedes the earlier assessment in September.

Historical requests retain their January information cutoff and model hash. Review, approval and settlement gates are exercised. Duplicate events have one effect. Local replay reproduces the history even after a separately fitted version 2 is registered in 2025. A changed event-state hash fails replay. This is local historical assessment processing, not an external approval or booking system.

Twenty out-of-time obligors with frozen PD 0.10 have two observed defaults and complete 365-day follow-up: observed rate 0.10 and Brier score 0.09. Three separate earlier ten-obligor cohorts mature in February–April 2024 with 2, 3 and 4 defaults; their observed-minus-predicted residuals feed EWMA. Its hand path is 0.05, 0.125, 0.2125. Nineteen disclosed null paths define an illustrative prespecified monitoring threshold; no externally validated false-alarm claim is made. Feature Wasserstein distance is 3 under explicitly exchangeable rows.

An observed multistate cohort distinguishes state occupancy from first default: cure reduces default occupancy without removing historical first default. A separate competing-risk fit has one default and two exits among eight obligors; at year one its default incidence is (1-exp(-3/8))/3 and exit incidence twice that. No independent-cause probability is added as though it were a competing-risk cumulative incidence.

Restructuring candidates are conditional scenarios with explicit uptake, costs and cashflows, not causal treatment effects. The original claim defaults before the scheduled repayment, retaining EAD 80. Realized recovery 60 less costs 4 gives closed LGD 0.30. The same incomplete ledger does not produce a final observed LGD. Separate historical terminal-settlement data fit a cure/positive-recovery/timing hurdle model; open claims use conditional remaining resolution times and an explicit undiscounted tail. The ultimate LGD oracle is 1 - (10 + 80*(1+exp(0.125))/3)/100 = 0.3311604125. Default and restructuring losses use mutually exclusive joint states, yielding expected loss 3.4 without double counting.

The portfolio contains exposures 80 and 100 in distinct sectors; HHI is 41/81. Observed loss validation checks closed settlements and correct default-date exposures, with EAD-weighted LGD bias 1/30 and equal-weight EAD bias -5. Fully drawn lines have undefined CCF denominators rather than invented zero CCFs.

Both languages genuinely fit their own models. Independent Weibull optimizers differ slightly: each censored log-likelihood is within 2e-8 of an independent profiled score-root optimum. Eight within-grid/tail timing fields have individually documented relative tolerance 1e-4; all remaining numeric fields use 1e-12 absolute/1e-10 relative tolerance, with exact nonnumeric comparison. Both original fitted artifacts are also packaged as integrity-checked JSON and replayed in both languages. Their forecasts and model hashes meet the strict comparison without exceptions. No floating-point rounding hides differences.

The corporate component and lifecycle together exercise all 41 required recipe/phase operations. This count supplements the independent financial, cohort, date, model-selection and replay checks; call counts alone are not acceptance evidence.

Optional audit access: the corporate governance helper accepts return_audit_artifacts; specialized governance accepts it on its public example runner and journey helper. When true, the existing report is accompanied by the actual frozen registry, original/history requests and their actual results. Default reports remain exactly unchanged.
