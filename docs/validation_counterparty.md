# Counterparty exposure validation

`counterparty_exposure_backtest(forecasts, observations, quantile_levels=..., design=..., uncertainty=...)` assesses physical exposure distributions per counterparty/netting set and separately assesses mature observed default closeouts. It consumes actual sealed path artifacts from `aggregate_netting_set_exposure` and `simulate_collateral_margin`; closeouts are recalculated with `closeout_exposure_at_default`.

The terminology follows the distinction between legally bounded netting sets, exposure distributions, physical versus risk-neutral measures, and margin-period closeout in [BCBS CRE50, published 2024-07-05](https://www.bis.org/committees/bcbs/basel-framework/standard/cre/50/inforce/2019-12-15/published/2024-07-05), particularly 50.15, 50.19 and 50.22–50.28. The validator is an explicit methodological check, not a regulatory capital engine or legal determination.

Forecast rows carry the shared validation identities: `prediction_id`, `entity_id`, `cluster_id`, `segment`, `as_of`, `forecast_known_at`, `horizon_end`, `model_id`, `model_version`, `parameter_version`. Add `counterparty_id`, `netting_set_id`, `legal_version`, `exposure_target` (UNCOLLATERALIZED or COLLATERALIZED), `netting_paths`, optional `weight`. A collateralized target also needs `collateral_paths` and `csa_version`. The design declares those model/parameter identities, `data_fingerprint`, `population_id`, `split_id`, HOLDOUT/EXTERNAL `prediction_origin`, `evaluation_cutoff`, `currency` and a PHYSICAL target horizon. Artifacts start at forecast as-of, use the same currency and retain historically known legal/CSA contracts.

`path_weights` optionally maps every scenario ID to a nonnegative mass; otherwise weights are equal. They are normalized stably. The exact horizon-date/netting-set slice supplies expected exposure and weighted empirical inverse-CDF quantiles. An exception is strictly greater than its quantile. With atoms, realized exception probability need not equal one minus the level; the report shows the model's actual strict-tail mass separately. Quantile pinball loss and weighted mean forecast bias/MAE/RMSE are reported. Risk-neutral distributions are not silently tested as physical frequencies.

Observation records match `prediction_id` and declare `observation_status` OBSERVED or CENSORED. Observed market values require `observed_at` exactly equal to horizon-end, subsequent `known_at`, `observed_path_id`, the actual sealed netting/collateral path artifacts, unchanged legal/CSA versions and `reported_exposure`. No future-known observation is scored. The validator compares the full netting-agreement and margin-policy contents to the forecast, selects the exact realized path/set/date, and reconciles reported exposure with the artifact's per-set value. Netting across counterparties or sets cannot reduce that value. Reconciliation conflicts fail the contract check and exclude the affected market observation.

Optional default-closeout forecasts declare `default_events`, `intermediate_cashflows`, `freeze_timing`, `cashflow_convention`, and `default_sampling_contract` with `mode='JOINT_SIMULATED_PATHS'`, historically available `known_at`, and `reference`. Events refer to the same frozen joint market/default scenario IDs. Conditional mean closeout exposure uses only positive-weight simulated defaults inside the forecast horizon. It is not computed by multiplying a marginal exposure distribution by an unrelated default frequency.

An observed default closeout adds `closeout_event`, `intermediate_cashflows`, `reported_closeout_exposure`, `closeout_known_at` to its observation. Closeout date, knowledge date and forecast horizon must all be mature at evaluation. The public closeout routine freezes collateral units at the declared default-time boundary and revalues those units at closeout, applying the posted-return-claim terms and unsettled cashflow convention. A complete closeout can be scored even when the ordinary horizon market observation is censored. Missing defaults are never zero-loss labels. With no simulated default support or no observed mature closeout, the conditional metric is non-applicable.

For an independent hand case, net value at closeout is 90, unpaid contractual cash is 10, and 40 units frozen at default are worth 0.5 each at closeout. Closeout exposure is `90 + 10 - 40*0.5 = 80`, even if contemporaneous margin has reduced current market exposure to zero. Separately, equal-weight physical samples 0,20,40,80 have mean35, empirical median20 and 95th percentile80.

Use no uncertainty or supplied whole-cluster bootstrap draws. The same resampled counterparties/time-dependence clusters are applied to exposure, exceptions and conditional-closeout metrics. Intervals condition on frozen simulation paths; Monte Carlo model-estimation uncertainty is not included. A resample with no default observation makes that interval non-estimable. These checks do not establish a rare-default loss distribution, LGD, causal effects, CVA or model approval.
