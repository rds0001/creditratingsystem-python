# Historical decision-policy validation

`decision_policy_backtest(evaluated_grid, grid_inputs, historical_cases, *, design, reference_candidate_id, uncertainty='none', cluster_resamples=None, confidence_level=.95, tolerance=1e-10, context=None)` audits a sealed `DecisionGrid` and the exact arguments originally supplied to `evaluate_decision_grid`. The grid is evaluated locally again and compared recursively. This replays fixed policies and economics; it neither fits models nor selects a policy or executes lending actions. Design target, currency, data fingerprint, split and cutoff must match. All candidates must use the same cases and predeclared scenario constraints.

Each historical case uses the standard dated validation row fields and also binds `case_id`, `historical_candidate_id`, sealed `historical_decision`, Boolean `offered` and `taken_up`, `stages_known_at`, explicit nullable `offered_limit` and `booked_amount`. Optional actual application costs require `application_cost`, `cost_complete` and `cost_known_at`. Actual funding and credit outcomes are the original grid's `observed_outcomes`, with an explicit funded or unfunded record for every case. Funding requires take-up, which requires an offer. Funded records retain the actual funding-date target horizon. Unfunded credit labels are invalid, not nondefaults. Stage rates use only stages known by cutoff; application costs use known complete records, with their own denominator.

Offered cases require a frozen candidate offer with `contract_terms.limit`; further price, tenor and currency terms should be included in that object. Whole offer content is hashed, so changing a limit, price or other term prevents reuse of the old booked outcome as a matching offer. An actual offer without local policy approval, a limit above its frozen cap, booked principal above the offered limit, differing booked terms, expired historical policy or altered historical recommendation is reported as a violation. Schema 1.1 applicability and additional categorical gates are checked by the actual policy evaluator, including investment/counterparty-specific affordability applicability.

The output separates factual offer/take-up/funding rates and mature matched-funded risk from expected scenario economics. Candidate-minus-reference differences are computed casewise on common weights. Whole-cluster bootstrap draws give actual percentile intervals for those paired scenario contrasts. They do not estimate uncertainty in supplied take-up, PD, LGD, NPV assumptions, nor identify a policy treatment effect. A violated predeclared scenario constraint blocks this supplied candidate set; arithmetic agreement alone has status `NOT_ASSESSED`, not policy approval.

For two equally weighted applications, approving one at take-up 0.5, conditional NPV 10 and offer cost 1 gives expected NPV per application 2. Approving both gives 4, hence a paired difference of 2. Conditional expected loss 2 gives respective application-level losses 0.5 and 1. Actual funding of only the first application gives funding rate 0.5; one observed default among that funded case gives observed PD 1. The second approval does not invent an observed repayment. Cluster draws selecting each case twice give differences 0 and 4 and a 95% type-7 interval [0.1, 3.9].

Full-policy causal value is explicitly unidentified. A frozen supported-offer assumption, or a changed limit with the same borrower, is not an off-policy identification contract. Dedicated causal estimation would require its own treatment definition, exchangeability/positivity/consistency evidence and estimator. The missing financed-outcome distinction follows [Ehrhardt et al.](https://adimajo.github.io/assets/publications/rejectInference.pdf); see [decision evaluation](decisioning_evaluation.md) for the fixed policy-grid contracts.

Repeated observations of one entity must remain in the same sampling cluster. Future-known actual stages do not contribute compliance violations at an earlier cutoff. Repeated-entity and cluster counts accompany the result. All computed scenario and factual metrics must remain finite.

The optional evaluation-profile `target_contract_version="1.1"` requires the complete six-field target, including `performance_origin="first_draw_at"`, and admits `default` or `first_default` without renaming either event. The validation design must explicitly repeat `policy_target_contract_version="1.1"` and the same performance origin; its ordinary five-field target is reconstructed and compared with the complete profile/policy/offer contract. Missing version or `"1.0"` preserves the existing default-only behavior; unknown versions and other origins/events are rejected. This extension does not widen quote/pricing target gates. Incomplete realized net-profit/loss observations remain unknown even when actual approval, uptake and funding stages are observed.
